Google Play — Apply for Production Access
==========================================

HOW DID YOU RECRUIT USERS FOR YOUR CLOSED TEST?
-------------------------------------------------
Most of them were friends and family members and some were friends of friends.


HOW EASY WAS IT TO RECRUIT TESTERS FOR YOUR APP?
-------------------------------------------------
Difficult


DESCRIBE THE ENGAGEMENT YOU RECEIVED FROM TESTERS DURING YOUR CLOSED TEST
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Testers rode in groups of 2–5 and used live GPS, Push-to-Talk voice, group chat, and SOS on real rides. All major features were exercised. Usage matched real-world expectations — joining sessions, tracking group position, and communicating hands-free while riding.


PROVIDE A SUMMARY OF THE FEEDBACK THAT YOU RECEIVED FROM TESTERS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Feedback collected via a shared WhatsApp group. Key issues: PTT hold-to-talk was unsafe while riding; audio dropped on mobile data; SOS required a confirm tap; Ride ID sharing was confusing. All issues were addressed in v1.2 before applying for production.


ABOUT YOUR APP
==============

WHO IS THE INTENDED AUDIENCE OF YOUR APP?
------------------------------------------
Motorcycle riders who participate in group rides — recreational weekend riders, touring clubs, and riding communities. Users are adults who need hands-free communication and live group coordination while riding, without stopping to check their phone or make calls.


DESCRIBE HOW YOUR APP PROVIDES VALUE TO USERS
----------------------------------------------
BikerSync eliminates the need to stop or look at a phone during a group ride. Live GPS keeps the group together, PTT voice lets riders communicate hands-free, and one-tap SOS provides instant emergency alerting — all in one app built for the realities of riding.


HOW MANY INSTALLS DO YOU EXPECT IN YOUR FIRST YEAR?
----------------------------------------------------
0 – 10K


YOUR PRODUCTION READINESS
==========================

WHAT CHANGES DID YOU MAKE TO YOUR APP BASED ON WHAT YOU LEARNED DURING YOUR CLOSED TEST?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Redesigned PTT as tap-to-toggle with a group voice channel; switched to WebRTC+TURN for reliable audio over mobile data; removed the SOS confirmation dialog; simplified Ride ID sharing; fixed a Wi-Fi Direct loop that caused repeated permission popups.


HOW DID YOU DECIDE THAT YOUR APP IS READY FOR PRODUCTION?
----------------------------------------------------------
All tester-reported issues were fixed and verified on real Android devices. The app ran through full ride sessions — GPS tracking, PTT, chat, and SOS — without crashes. Core flows are stable across mobile data and Wi-Fi, and Firebase security rules are deployed.
